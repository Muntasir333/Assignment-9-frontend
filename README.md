# 🏟️ Sportnest - Sports Facility Reservation Platform

A modern **full-stack sports facility reservation platform** that allows users to discover sports facilities, check availability, and book their preferred sports venues easily.

Sportnest provides a seamless booking experience for users while allowing facility owners to manage their sports facilities, availability, and reservations efficiently.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://sportnest-eosin.vercel.app/

---

# 🎯 Project Overview

Finding and booking sports facilities can often be inconvenient due to limited information, manual communication, and unclear availability.

**Sportnest** solves this problem by providing a centralized platform where users can explore different sports facilities, view details, select available time slots, and make reservations.

The platform also provides management features for facility owners to add, update, and control their facilities.

- 🔍 Browse available sports facilities

  - Price per hour
